Stacked bead pendant lights in Mumbai

These 10 pendant lights stack glass beads, opal forms and sculpted glass along a single drop. Colours range from blush, peach, aqua and pastel blue to smoke, opal and amber, and the fittings come in matte black or brushed gold.

The slim, vertical shape can suit tall spaces such as a double-height room or a stairwell, and narrow spots such as beside a bed. See the designs at the Art Lights showroom in Lohar Chawl, or send an enquiry for a single piece or a bulk order.
