Linear pendant lights in Mumbai

Linear pendants hang several shades at varied heights from one long, rectangular or oval canopy. This range of 10 includes mirrored copper and amber glass, smoke and opal glass, frosted glass leaves and a sweeping crystal oval ring, in matte black or gold.

The long canopy can suit dining tables and kitchen islands. See them at the Art Lights showroom in Lohar Chawl, Mumbai, or send an enquiry for a single piece or a bulk order.
